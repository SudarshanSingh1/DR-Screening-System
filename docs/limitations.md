## 1. Algorithmic & Deep Learning Limitations

### 1.1 Single-Model Production Scope
While a modular six-model architecture is documented for future research, the current deployed system relies on a single **EfficientNetB0 V2** classifier. It does not perform deep semantic segmentation of retinal microvasculature (U-Net++) or individual microaneurysms (DeepLabV3+).

### 1.2 Heuristic Nature of Retinal Evidence Overlays
The four visual evidence previews generated by [`inference_service/evidence_generator.py`](file:///Users/sudarshankumar/DR-Screening-System/inference_service/evidence_generator.py) (vessel preview, landmark markers, lesion highlight map) are **exploratory spatial image-processing filters** (Gaussian high-pass, green channel inversion, edge thresholding). They are **not** deep learning segmentations and must not be used as clinical ground truth.

### 1.3 Uncalibrated Softmax Probabilities
Output probabilities $[p_0..p_4]$ represent normalized exponential logits from the final dense layer. Because neural networks with cross-entropy loss tend to be overconfident, these values are uncalibrated and do not represent true Bayesian posterior probabilities. Post-hoc temperature scaling ($T$) is planned to minimize Expected Calibration Error (ECE).

### 1.4 Benchmark Verification Cohort Performance
On the 15-sample held-out verification split (`model/production/V2/verification_report.json`), the deployed model achieves a sensitivity of $22.2\%$ ($2/9$) and specificity of $50.0\%$ ($3/6$). While historical development splits achieved higher accuracy, this empirical result illustrates the danger of false negatives and underscores why **Doctor-in-the-Loop review is mandatory**.

## 2. Clinical & Operational Boundaries

### 2.1 Non-Autonomous Research Prototype
Vision AI does not have CDSCO (India) or FDA clearance. It is an assistive decision-support research prototype created for Smart India Hackathon 2026. Certified ophthalmologist sign-off is required on every case before any clinical action.

### 2.2 Co-Morbid Retinal Pathology Blindspots
The model is trained specifically on diabetic retinopathy. It does not screen for Age-Related Macular Degeneration (AMD), Central Retinal Vein Occlusion (CRVO), Glaucomatous optic neuropathy, or retinal detachments.

### 2.3 Store-and-Forward Edge Synchronization
The edge deployment currently requires active LAN connectivity between the web client and Docker host. Asynchronous offline queue synchronization to tertiary hospitals during complete backhaul network failures is an active research roadmap item.
